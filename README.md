* 📅 **Major Life Events**: A timeline showcasing key milestones from 2004 to my first job in 2026.
* 🏆 **Achievements**: Records of clearing the TCS NQT, participating in hackathons, and daily coding practice.
* 🔬 **Research Projects**: Interest areas focusing on AI applications in Automation, Agriculture, and Healthcare.
* 💻 **Technical Skills**: Core competencies in Java, HTML, CSS, and SQL.
* 🧰 **Tools & Environments**: Hands-on experience with VS Code, GitHub, and Eclipse.
* 🎨 **Hobbies**: Chess, reading, and travelling.
* 📚 **Tech Gallery**: Descriptive lists defining key web and database technologies.
* 🎯 **Personal Development Plan**: Short-term goals (Mastering Java, Full Stack) and long-term goals (building a startup, creating employment opportunities).

## 📐 Technologies Used

* **HTML5**: Semantic tags, ordered and unordered lists, definition lists (`<dl>`, `<dt>`, `<dd>`), and structuring elements.
* **Git & GitHub**: Version control and hosting.
